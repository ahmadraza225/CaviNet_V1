## Intended use

- **Decision support** for doctors: for a patient already suspected of mycobacterial lung disease, whether the chest CT looks more like **TB** or **NTM**, with a calibrated confidence.
- Output: probability of TB; predicted class TB if p ≥ 0.5, otherwise NTM; confidence = probability of the predicted class; band High ≥ 80.0%, Moderate ≥ 65.0%, otherwise Low (shown as Inconclusive).
- Always confirmed by laboratory tests. Decision support only. Not a diagnosis.

## Not intended for

- Diagnosing TB, screening healthy people, or any disease other than TB vs NTM.
- Detecting, outlining or locating cavities or other lesions.
- Identifying NTM species.
- Use in other hospitals without local validation.

## Preprocessing (section 11.1)

Identical in training and in the application (stored in the model file):

- Clip below -1024.0 HU; lung mask with lungmask R231 (fallback to the body outline below 500.0 mL, recorded as a warning).
- Resample to 1.5 mm; crop to the lungs + 10.0 mm; lung window [-1350.0, 150.0] HU scaled to 0-1; resize to 128 × 128 × 128 voxels (float16).

## Training (section 11.4)

- BCE with logits, TB positive, pos_weight = #NTM / #TB of each training fold; AdamW (lr 0.0001, weight decay 0.0001), 3 warm-up epochs then cosine.
- Up to 60 epochs, early stopping on validation AUC (patience 12), best checkpoint kept; batch 4 (effective 16), mixed precision on the GPU.
- Augmentation: left-right flip p 0.5, rotation ±10.0°, scale [0.9, 1.1], translation ±8.0 voxels, intensity ±10.0%, Gaussian noise σ 0.01; seed 42.

## Limitations

- Single-centre data (one hospital in China): performance may differ elsewhere; there is no external validation set.
- Only patients with TB or NTM were in the data: do not use it for patients without suspected mycobacterial lung disease, for screening, or for other lung diseases.
- It does not detect or locate cavities or other lesions.
- Labels come from the dataset's diagnoses; any errors in them are inherited.
- TB and NTM overlap substantially on CT; perfect separation is not expected.
- Research prototype only: not a medical device and not clinically validated.

## Ethical considerations

- Trained on public, anonymised data (CC BY 4.0); no patient data from Pakistani hospitals.
- Uploaded scans are de-identified on arrival; every result carries the disclaimer.
- All results, including unfavourable ones, are reported honestly.
